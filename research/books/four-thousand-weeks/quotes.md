## Ch. 3 — Facing Finitude

- "The original Latin word for 'decide' — decidere — means 'to cut off,' as in slicing away alternatives; it’s a close cousin of words like 'homicide' and 'suicide'." — Ch. 3 (Notion body)
- "I’m building a life—but at one and the same time, I’m closing off the possibility [of] countless others, forever." — Ch. 3 (Notion body)
- "…living a truly authentic life—becoming fully human—means facing up [to] that fact [of finitude]." (สรุปประเด็น Heidegger จาก Notion body) — Ch. 3 (Notion body)
- "The exhilaration that sometimes arises when you grasp this truth about finitude has been called the 'joy of missing out,' by way of a deliberate contrast with the idea of the 'fear of missing out.'" — Ch. 3 (Notion body)
