# Gate answers & CUT-scan mechanics (Benz rounds)

Topical depth for `content-pipeline-sessions`. SKILL.md is over the 100k char limit — new round-mechanics lessons land here until a curator consolidation moves them up.

## CUT-substring hygiene — swap the carrier word before declaring a FP

A zero-count CUT term can trip inside an ordinary word it has no business guarding — the draft battery and the Notion forbidden-scan both match raw substrings, and three strikes can land in a single rewrite round (พร sits inside ฝั่ง / ภาพรวม / เพราะงั้น).

When a free synonym exists, swap the word (ดังนั้น for เพราะงั้น, สรุปสั้นที่สุด for ภาพรวมสั้นที่สุด, สินค้าที่วางขาย for สินค้าฝั่งที่ขาย) — every added FP declaration is permanent verifier debt that weakens the forbidden-scan for every future round; declare a FP only when no synonym carries the same meaning (lesson 22's "never fix" applies to INTENTIONAL compounds like มาพร้อมกัน, not to accidental carriers). For a position-specific compound a backreference guard beats a rewrite: `(?<!มา)พร` keeps มาพร้อม out of the count while still catching a bare พร — and classify every remaining hit in context before acting on the number, because a raw count either blocks a clean draft or hides a real violation inside a dismissed number.

Gate: before adding a term to the FP declaration, hunt a synonym; before shipping, grep the draft for every CUT substring and check each hit's carrier word — fix the word, not the list.

## Owner sovereignty over gates — serving the answer can mean a full frame rebuild

When the owner's gate answer contradicts the critic's recommendation, the critic's post-gate spec follows the OWNER's answer verbatim — and that can mean rebuilding the WHOLE reading frame (a fresh โครง, a new spine sentence verbatim, a re-scoped closer), not just a one-line closer swap.

Grade the resulting publish against the keys of the critic's LAST REAL round, and re-scope the CUT / dead-vocab list to the new frame before checking: an axis that was the spine two rounds ago can be legitimately retired by the owner's gate answer — a verify-0 on the old spine vocabulary is then correct, not a violation.

Related: grade each key BY DEFINITION (already in SKILL.md round-type selector); the frame rebuild just moves what the definitions permit.

## Same-lane guard covers form templates; an owner terminology steer is a whole-draft swap

Before filing any draft, check its quote and title-tail against a recently-published same-lane sibling's verbatim lines IN CODE (shared-bigram / shared-clause-template check) and re-word the new draft off the sibling's template — two posts in one feed reading as one voice-template is a repeat even when every word differs, and a vocabulary-only scan cannot catch it. An owner terminology steer (a body word → its technical/English form) is all-or-nothing: sweep the title, every body line, and the header notes for every surviving form of the old term in the SAME pass — a half-swapped draft is exactly what the next critique round opens with.
