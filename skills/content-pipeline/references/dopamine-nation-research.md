# Dopamine Nation (Anna Lembke) — verified research notes

## Book anchors
- Dr. Anna Lembke = Stanford psychiatrist, addiction specialist. Book: "Dopamine Nation: Finding Balance in the Age of Information" (2021).
- **D.O.P.A.M.I.N.E. protocol (Part 3, "Dopamine Homeostasis")** — 8-step, 30-day reset for cheap-dopamine addiction. Verified via secondary sources 2026-09-10 (linkedin rakesh-mullassery insight post: "A.M.I.N.E guide by Anna Lembke for 30 DAYS to break your addiction cycle"; leapaheadapp.com/en/blog/how-to-do-a-dopamine-detox full step list):
  - **D-Data**: identify the drug, Screen Time/credit card facts, quantify to stop self-denial
  - **O-Objectives**: what the behavior is doing for you (escape anxiety / numb loneliness) — the emotional void
  - **P-Problems**: list consequences (poor sleep, deadlines, health, family) — write on physical paper
  - **A-Abstinence**: COMPLETE break for set period; "moderation does not work for a hijacked brain" (can't tell alcoholic one beer / TikToker 5 min); 30 days for neuroplastic change
  - **M-Mindfulness**: pain gremlins scream (bored, anxious); observe cravings without acting; it will pass
  - **I-Insight**: brain fog lifts; see wasted time + what behavior was masking
  - **N-Next Steps**: structured re-engagement plan — strict time limits + physical barriers ("cannot avoid screens forever")
  - **E-Experiment**: A/B test what works (email 2x/day OK, Twitter on phone = failure); adjust environment
- **BANNED during fast** (leapahead guide): all social media (delete apps), infinite scroll (YouTube/TikTok/news), video games, streaming, hyper-palatable food (fast food/sugar/alcohol), porn. **ALLOWED/encouraged**: physical books (no audiobooks-as-escape), pen & paper journaling, exercise (weights/run/yoga), long walks, real conversation.
- Homeostasis concept: brain has a set-point for pleasure; chronic high-dopamine pushes it up → ordinary tasks stop registering (reward sensor re-calibrated to high voltage). Recovery = set-point reset over weeks.
