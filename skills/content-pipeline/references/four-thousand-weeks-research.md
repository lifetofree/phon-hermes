## Verified quotes (all 3 from user idea, confirmed via multiple secondary sources)

3. "The internet makes this all much more agonizing because it promises to help you make better use of your time, while simultaneously exposing you to vastly more potential uses for your time" — tobsysinclair.com, mattswain.com/booknotes/four-thousand-weeks, bymatthart.com/4thousand

## Book facts
- Hook: "The average human lifespan is absurdly, terrifyingly, insultingly short." (first sentence, repeated on last page) — NYT review 2021-08-11
- ~4,000 weeks average lifespan; ~4,000 of each day-of-week (Guardian review)
- Core thesis: time management can't work because the goal (fit everything in) is unachievable; embrace limits/mortality instead
- JOMO framing: peace from accepting you can't do everything — NOT from doing everything
