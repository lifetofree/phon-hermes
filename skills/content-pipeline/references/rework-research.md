## Framing (what Rework actually says)
- Interruption is the ENEMY of productivity (not collaboration per se). Long stretches of uninterrupted "alone time" = where deep/meaningful work happens.
- Meetings are toxic: drift, need prep, one moron, procreate. Fix: timer, fewest people, clear agenda, start with a problem, end with a solution + owner.
- Default to asynchronous communication; reserve synchronous time for decisions only.
- Momentum fuels motivation — ship small things (quick wins) to keep pace; scope > resources.
- Rework is a manifesto, evidence is anecdotal (Goodreads) — cite it as "Fried & DHH wrote / argued", not as peer-reviewed science.
