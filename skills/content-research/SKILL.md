# Content Research (adduckivity)

## When to Use

Load this when the job is the INPUT leg of an adduckivity pair: researching a topic/product/book before drafting, filing research notes, managing the research vault + books library, or mining YouTube transcripts as sources. Drafting belongs to `content-pipeline`; shipping pages belongs to `content-notion-ops`.

## Where research lives

- **SSOT:** `~/hermes-agent/research/` — one file per topic, `<slug>-research.md`, front-matter: `type: research` / `topic:` / `used_by: [[pair-0NN]]`. NEVER create research files inside a skill's `references/` again — the 26 files there are symlinks into research/ kept so old paths resolve.
- **Books:** `research/books/catalog.md` is GENERATED by `content-pipeline/scripts/sync_books_library.py` (syncs the Notion "Books Library" DB; chapter lists from child_database rows, `heading_3 Chapter #N` fallback; cached by `last_edited_time`). Per-book folders are hand-created only when a book goes active. **Quote-provenance gate: a post may cite a book quote only after it lands in `research/books/<slug>/quotes.md`** — catalog is generated, never hand-edited, and a book with no chapters stays blank (never fabricate chapters).
- **Layout contract + node types:** `content-pipeline/references/research-vault.md` (directory tree, generated vs curated vault nodes, RESEARCH_LINKS wiring).

## Research standards (learned the hard way — keep all of them)

- **Primary source first:** product/docs/blog/repo of the maker before any press. Re-verify facts (prices, license, versions) ON THE SHIP DAY — pricing and plans decay in weeks (ZCode pricing re-checked 28 Oct, stripped from the post 9 days later on owner order).
- **Unit vocabulary discipline:** a product's quota unit is whatever the OWNER company calls it — GLM/ZCode bills in tokens, AutoClaw talks credits; mixing unit vocabularies in one post is a factual error even when every number is individually right (pair #103: three strip-rounds to get units out entirely). When the unit cannot be verified, the claim goes — not reworded.
- **News events:** qualitative facts only in the body (who/what/which house), result numbers stay out; cite ≥2 independent outlets; the event date stays in Arabic numerals as the anchor.
- **Evidence classes drive verb strength:** first-look vs REAL-USAGE (e.g. 125 วัน of hands-on) decides which verbs the draft may carry — usage-duration is exposure evidence, never quality evidence.
- **Books as source:** quote only with page/provenance from quotes.md; ideas (map-territory, chess chunking, dissonance) get the book named once with a Thai gloss, not a quote dump.

## Vault integration

- After any pair close or index change: `python3 content-pipeline/scripts/generate_vault.py` (hash-gated, idempotent — refreshes pair files + MOC `index.md`).
- Pair→research back-links: add one key to `RESEARCH_LINKS` in the generator; research-side `used_by` lives in the file's front-matter.
- Obsidian opens at repo root `~/hermes-agent/` (it does not follow symlinks inside a nested vault root — opening `content-study/vault` alone hides research/).

## YouTube transcripts

Recipe in `content-pipeline/references/youtube-transcript.md`: disambiguate via oembed, fetch with youtube-transcript-api v2 (`list()` + `fetch()` — the old `get_transcript`/`list_transcripts` classmethods are gone), save under references/, then mine.
